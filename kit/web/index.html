NOMAD Command Center

Offline knowledge kit. Everything here runs from this Mac and the EXTREME PRO drive, with no internet needed.

Library …

Full Wikipedia with images, medical wiki, post-disaster guides, water, food, knots, iFixit repair guides, Wikivoyage.

AI assistant …

Local model on the Mac's GPU through Ollama. Private and offline.

Education …

Kolibri with Khan Academy (US curriculum): videos and exercises, K–12 and up.

Guides

First aid and survival manuals, BTECH UV-PRO radio and frequency guides, FEMA, Las Vegas heat and flood, San Antonio winter storms.

Maps …

Street-level offline maps: US West (incl. Las Vegas and Henderson) and greater San Antonio, TX.

If something is down

  1. Plug in the EXTREME PRO drive and double-click Start NOMAD.command on it.
  2. Library: in the Kiwix app, click Hotspot in the sidebar, select all the books, and click Start Hotspot.
  3. Education: on first visit, Kolibri asks you to create a facility and admin account.
