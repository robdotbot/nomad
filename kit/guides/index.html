Emergency guides

Survival and first aid

  • Army ATP 4-02.11: Casualty Response and First Aid (2026)Current Army first-aid manual for non-medics: bleeding, airway, burns, fractures, shock.
  • Multi-Service Survival ManualWater, food, shelter, fire, navigation, signaling. Desert, cold and other environments.

Radio: BTECH UV-PRO

  • BTECH UV-PRO user manual (Rev 3)The radio itself: buttons, menus, channels, power, IP67 care, GPS, airband receive, APRS settings on the radio.
  • BTECH app manual (UV-PRO, 2025)Bluetooth pairing and the phone app: programming channels, APRS setup (callsign, location sharing, digipeater), GPS sharing, text messages.

Radio: Baofeng UV-5R Mini

  • Baofeng UV-5R Mini user manualButtons, menus, channels, scanning, NOAA weather alerts, wireless programming with the OLA Radio app.
  • Miklor: UV-5R Mini review and notesCommunity notes: CHIRP programming, quirks, settings worth changing. Saved web page.
  • UV-5R Mini FCC certificateCertified frequency ranges and power.

Programming software is on the drive at installers/radio/: CHIRP for Mac (needs Rosetta and a K1 FTDI cable) and Baofeng's Windows CPS.

Radio: field guides

  • CISA NIFOG 2.02: National Interoperability Field Operations GuideThe responder's frequency bible: national interoperability, ham calling and emergency frequencies, NOAA weather radio, aviation and marine channels, MURS/GMRS/FRS, phonetic alphabet.
  • ARRL ARES Field Resources Manual (2019)Emergency ham operating: net procedure, message forms (ICS-213, radiogram), go-kit checklists.
  • ARRL US amateur bands chartWhich frequencies your license class can transmit on. APRS in the US: 144.390 MHz.

Airband (receive only)

  • FAA Chart Supplement: Southwest (Oct 29, 2026)Tower, ground, ATIS and approach frequencies for Nevada airports, incl. Las Vegas (LAS) and Henderson (HND). Search for the airport name.
  • FAA Chart Supplement: South Central (Oct 29, 2026)Same for Texas, incl. San Antonio (SAT). Replaced every 56 days; the frequencies rarely change.

General preparedness

  • FEMA: Are You Ready? In-Depth Guide to Citizen PreparednessKits, family plans, and what to do before, during and after each kind of disaster. 2004 edition, still FEMA's most complete guide.

Bible

  • King James Version (KJV)1611/1769 Protestant classic, with the Apocrypha.
  • World English Bible (WEB)Modern English, public domain. Includes the Deuterocanon used by Catholic and Orthodox churches.
  • American Standard Version (ASV, 1901)Literal Protestant translation; basis of many modern versions.
  • Douay-Rheims (Challoner)Traditional Catholic English Bible, from the Latin Vulgate.
  • Brenton SeptuagintGreek Old Testament in English, as used by Eastern Orthodox churches.

Public-domain editions from eBible.org. Modern copyrighted versions (NIV, ESV, NKJV, NASB) can't be freely downloaded.
